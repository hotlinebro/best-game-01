# Lumenhollow

**Genre:** light-zone tower defense / wave survival.

Guard a moonwell through 8 escalating, finite waves. Place glow beacons around the well; each beacon illuminates a circle that damages shadow moths. Spend collected motes on upgrades or new beacons, choosing coverage and economy. Moths that reach the well drain its 10 integrity. Clear wave 8 to win; lose when integrity reaches zero. The opening build period lasts 10 seconds; cleared waves give 6 seconds to prepare. Each wave grants 25 motes, each kill 6, each beacon costs 30, and each upgrade costs 35.

Mouse click places/selects a beacon; keys 1/2 choose beacon/upgrade, U upgrades the selected beacon, P pauses, R restarts, M mutes. Start and restart are clickable or Space/Enter. HUD shows wave, integrity, motes, score, selected beacon, remaining moths, and next-wave countdown. Every shot, hit, kill, placement, upgrade, wave and ending has optional synthesized Web Audio feedback, unlocked on a user gesture. Canvas art includes stars, moonlight, winged moths with trails, beacon glows, beams, kill particles, mote labels and well damage flashes. Fresh browser screenshots must be taken after the fixes; the repository currently has only a screenshot placeholder.
