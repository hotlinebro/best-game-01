# Lumenhollow

**Genre:** light-zone tower defense / wave survival.

Guard a wandering moonwell through 8 escalating waves. Place glow beacons around the well; each beacon illuminates a circle that damages shadow moths. Spend collected motes on upgrades or new beacons, choosing coverage and economy. Moths that reach the well drain its 10 integrity. Survive wave 8 to win; lose when integrity reaches zero.

Mouse click places/selects a beacon; keys 1/2 choose beacon/upgrade, U upgrades, P pauses, R restarts. Start and restart are clickable or Space/Enter. HUD shows wave, integrity, motes, score, and selected beacon. Every hit, kill, placement and wave has synthesized Web Audio feedback. Screenshots are taken after the build.
